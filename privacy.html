What we store

By default the studio keeps your projects and settings in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to our servers unless you enable cloud sync.

AI providers

If you connect a third-party AI provider (Groq, Gemini, OpenRouter), your prompt and API key are sent directly from your browser to that provider. They apply their own privacy terms.

Analytics

If enabled, analytics is privacy-respecting (Plausible/Umami). No cross-site tracking, no cookies.

Your rights

You can export all your data at any time from Settings → Export. Deleting projects removes them permanently.
