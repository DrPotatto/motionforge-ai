FAQ

Common questions

How does generation work?

Your prompt becomes a structured scene plan, then a validated animation spec. A deterministic Canvas 2D engine renders it — same result every time, no flaky frame captures.

Is it really free to start?

Yes. The local deterministic engine has no rate limits and no key. Connect a free-tier AI provider (Groq, Gemini, OpenRouter) when you want AI-directed scene planning.

What video format do I get?

Whatever your browser supports. Chrome and Edge produce WebM (VP9). Firefox produces WebM (VP8). MP4 is attempted when the browser reports support. Never faked.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything runs in your browser. No plugins, no CLI tools, no render farm.

Where are my projects stored?

In your browser's local storage by default. Upgrade to sync them to the cloud.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Subscriptions are month-to-month through our payment provider. Cancel from their billing portal.
